# FleXLube Lubrication System

- **Document type:** Technical news bulletin
- **Document ID:** TNB221
- **Issued:** April 2012
- **Product:** FleXLube
- **Status:** Reference

## Summary

FleXLube provides a central lubrication connection and zone concept for Emhart IS machine lines. Different zones can use different lubrication intervals and oil types.

## Main components

### Pump unit

- Oil reservoir
- Gear pump and motor
- Pressure relief valve
- Exhaust valve
- Controls

### Distribution box

- Isolation valve
- Solenoid-actuated gate valve
- Pressure switch
- Manometer

## Available configurations

- Single oil pump unit: `200-1891-8`
- Dual oil pump unit: `200-1901-8`
- Two-zone, single oil distribution box: `200-1895-1`
- Three-zone, single oil distribution box: `200-1895-2`
- Four-zone, single oil distribution box: `200-1895-3`
- Two-zone, dual oil feed: `200-1905-1`
- Three-zone, dual oil feed: `200-1905-2`
- Four-zone, dual oil feed: `200-1905-3` or `200-1905-4`

## Installation requirements

- Operating temperature: 5 C to 45 C
- Power supply: 400 VAC, 3-phase plus neutral, 50/60 Hz, 3 Amp, PE
- Piping and cable between pump and distribution boxes are not included in supply.

## Benefits

- No compressed-air installation or maintenance required
- Centralised zone control
- Shorter pipe runs
- Lower installation cost
- Independent timing for machine elements
- Potentially reduced oil consumption
