# Pantograph Baffle Troubleshooting Guide

- **Document type:** Troubleshooting guide
- **Document ID:** EG-TS-Baffle-001
- **Applies to:** Pantograph Baffle Arm 210-208-1
- **Audience:** Maintenance technicians
- **Status:** Prototype guidance

## Safety first

Stop the machine and follow site lockout and safety procedures before inspecting the baffle mechanism. Do not enter the machine area while moving equipment is active.

## Symptom: baffle does not align with blank mold

1. Confirm the machine type and center distance.
2. Check that the correct quick-change baffle holder is installed.
3. Inspect the eccentric pivot pin and mounting points.
4. Check the guide rod and upper/lower supports for looseness or damage.
5. Compare the assembly with the applicable installation drawing.
6. Escalate with machine type, serial number, photos, and the checks completed.

## Symptom: increased mechanism noise or wear

1. Stop operation if the noise indicates a safety risk.
2. Inspect pivot points, link assembly, and cam roller.
3. Check for contamination or insufficient lubrication.
4. Compare movement against the operating-principles guidance.
5. Record the affected section and operating conditions.
6. Request technical support if the cause cannot be confirmed.

## Information to include in a support request

- Machine type and serial number
- Affected section or cavity
- Part number and revision
- Error description and start time
- Photos or video of the mechanism
- Relevant drawing or document reference
- Checks already completed
