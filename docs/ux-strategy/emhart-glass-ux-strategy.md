# Emhart Glass UX Strategy Blueprint

**Source:** [Emhart Glass UX Strategy PDF](emhart-glass-ux-strategy.pdf)
**Purpose:** Define the UX problems, desired outcomes, strategy scope, principles, activities, and success measures for the Emhart Glass self-service portal.

## Strategy vision

> Empower Emhart Glass customers with a unified, intelligent self-service portal that reduces downtime, builds confidence, and drives operational excellence.

## Challenges

### Fragmented documentation
Information is scattered across systems and hard to find.

### Difficult spare-part identification
It is hard to find the right part and verify compatibility.

### Limited request visibility
Customers cannot easily track the status or history of requests.

### Slow support processes
Manual handoffs and inefficient workflows cause delays.

## Aspirations

### Reduce downtime
Help customers solve issues faster and keep operations running.

### Create a best-in-class self-service portal
Deliver an intuitive, modern experience customers love.

### Increase customer confidence
Make information reliable, accurate, and easy to trust.

### Improve operational efficiency
Streamline processes and reduce support workload.

## Focus areas

### Who we serve
- Maintenance technicians
- Buyers
- Plant managers
- Service coordinators

### What we focus on
- Documentation
- Parts
- Service requests
- Tracking and visibility

## Guiding principles

### Self-service first
Empower customers to find answers and solve problems on their own.

### Transparency
Provide clear visibility into status, information, and communication.

### Task-focused design
Design around user tasks and real-world workflows.

### Progressive disclosure
Show what is needed, when it is needed.

### One source of truth
Provide single, trusted, and up-to-date information across the portal.

## Strategy activities

### User research
Understand users, tasks, pain points, and needs.

### Personas
Define user types, goals, and key scenarios.

### Journey mapping
Map end-to-end customer and user journeys and pain points.

### Information architecture
Design an intuitive structure and navigation.

### Usability testing
Validate designs and improve the experience iteratively.

### Analytics and insights
Measure behaviour and uncover opportunities.

### Service blueprinting
Align people, processes, and systems to deliver the experience.

## Measurements

### Task success rate
Percentage of tasks completed successfully by users.

### Self-service resolution rate
Percentage of issues resolved without support intervention.

### RFQ-to-order conversion
Percentage of requests for quotation that convert to orders.

### Request resolution time
Average time to resolve service requests.

### Customer satisfaction
CSAT score from surveys and feedback.

## Product implications

- Make technical documentation searchable and easy to understand.
- Connect parts to equipment, machines, revisions, and compatibility information.
- Give customers a clear view of request, quotation, order, shipment, and service status.
- Reduce manual handoffs with structured workflows and clear ownership.
- Prioritise self-service while making escalation easy when self-service is insufficient.
- Design around the needs of technicians, buyers, plant managers, and service coordinators.
- Use journey mapping, usability testing, analytics, and service blueprinting as ongoing activities rather than one-time deliverables.
- Measure both user outcomes and operational outcomes.
