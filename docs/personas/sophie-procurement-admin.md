# Sophie

**Role:** Procurement & Admin Specialist
**Department:** Procurement & Supply Chain
**Source:** `Emhart Glass Persona Role - Sophie - Procurement & Admin.docx`

## Digital maturity
Medium-high (3.5/5). Comfortable using procurement and order management systems to track purchasing, suppliers, deliveries, and inventory information.

## Bio
Sophie coordinates the purchasing and delivery of spare parts and equipment. She manages quotes, orders, suppliers, and shipment tracking while ensuring internal stakeholders stay informed. Her focus is maintaining visibility throughout the ordering process and ensuring the right parts arrive when needed.

## Primary statement
> I want to ensure the right material arrives at the right place, at the right time, with complete visibility and minimal administrative effort.

## Goals
- Ensure material availability for maintenance and projects
- Obtain pricing and delivery commitments quickly
- Minimise procurement-related delays and risks
- Ensure compliance with customs and documentation requirements
- Reduce administrative effort through transparent processes
- Maintain visibility and control of the order lifecycle

## Key tasks
- Manage orders
- Request quotes
- Compare pricing
- Track shipments
- Coordinate deliveries
- Ensure parts availability

## Needs
- Reliable pricing, availability, and lead-time information
- End-to-end order and shipment tracking
- Proactive notifications and delivery updates
- Technical and customs documentation
- Efficient escalation path for critical orders
- Visibility into total cost of ownership and part lifecycle impact
- Confidence that ordered parts are compatible with installed equipment

## Pain points
- Poor order and shipment visibility
- Manual and email-driven processes
- Unclear pricing, stock, and lead times
- Customs and documentation delays
- Obsolete part numbers and replacement confusion

## Jobs to be done
Procure the right parts at the right time.

## Work environment
Office environment, procurement systems, and supplier interactions.
