# Tomas Nowak

**Role:** Emhart Glass Customer
**Source:** `Emhart Glass Persona - Tomas Nowak  Emhart Glass Customer.docx`

## Digital maturity
From highly technical power users to occasional portal users. Comfortable working with industrial systems and technical documentation, but expects digital tools to be intuitive, reliable, and efficient.

## Bio
Tomas is an experienced manufacturing professional with more than 15 years of experience in glass production and equipment maintenance. He is responsible for ensuring that production equipment remains reliable, maintainable, and cost-effective throughout its lifecycle. Tomas regularly interacts with Emhart Glass products, spare parts, repair services, training programmes, technical support, and digital tools. He relies on accurate information to make confident decisions that minimise downtime, optimise maintenance costs, and keep production running efficiently.

## Primary statement
> As an Emhart Glass customer, I want an easy self-service platform to operate and maintain my equipment, so that I can maximise uptime, reduce operational risk, and achieve my production goals.
