# Antonio

**Role:** Asset & Reliability Manager
**Department:** Maintenance & Reliability Engineering
**Source:** `Emhart Glass Persona Role - Antonio - Asset & Reliability Manager.docx`

## Digital maturity
Medium (3/5). Regularly uses asset management systems, maintenance analytics, and performance dashboards to monitor equipment health and support data-driven reliability decisions.

## Bio
Antonio is responsible for ensuring the reliability, performance, and lifecycle management of production equipment across one or more manufacturing sites. He develops maintenance strategies, plans investments, and uses asset data to minimise downtime, optimise costs, and extend equipment life. Antonio needs a clear overview of his installed base to make informed maintenance and replacement decisions.

## Primary statement
> I need full visibility of my equipment, maintenance risks, and installed base so I can prevent downtime, plan effectively, and make better maintenance decisions.

## Goals
- Annual maintenance planning
- Installed-base visibility
- Maintenance strategy decisions
- Multi-site equipment overview
- Compare assets and maintenance performance across plants
- Maintenance budgeting and lifecycle planning
- Reduce unplanned downtime
- Improve maintenance planning accuracy

## Key tasks
- Plan maintenance activities
- Monitor asset performance
- Analyse equipment reliability
- Optimise lifecycle costs
- Reduce downtime risk
- Define maintenance strategy

## Needs
- Central visibility of installed equipment, performance, and lifecycle status
- Machine-specific technical knowledge, service information, and documentation
- Reliable spare-part compatibility, availability, and lead-time information
- Data-driven maintenance planning and reliability insights
- Confidence that maintenance decisions use accurate installed-base data
- Predictive maintenance insights

## Pain points
- Fragmented asset information and no single source of truth
- Limited visibility into fleet-wide equipment status, configurations, and maintenance activities
- Reactive maintenance caused by insufficient lifecycle guidance
- Significant effort required to identify compatible spare parts and replacement options
- Lack of spare-part availability and lead-time information

## Jobs to be done
Optimise equipment reliability and lifecycle performance.

## Work environment
Plant office, maintenance workshops, and production floor.
