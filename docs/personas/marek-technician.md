# Marek

**Role:** Technician
**Department:** Maintenance Operations
**Source:** `Emhart Glass Persona Role- Marek - Technician.docx`

## Digital maturity
Medium (3/5). Uses digital documentation, maintenance systems, and troubleshooting tools daily to diagnose issues and identify the correct parts.

## Bio
Marek maintains and repairs production equipment to keep operations running smoothly. He spends much of his day diagnosing issues, finding the correct spare parts, and performing maintenance tasks. Marek relies on accurate machine-specific information, documentation, and troubleshooting guidance to resolve issues quickly and confidently.

## Primary statement
> I want to keep the machine running, complete tasks within available windows, and repair the machine as quickly as possible.

## Goals
- Restore equipment functionality quickly
- Minimise downtime and production disruption
- Identify the correct repair solution fast
- Complete maintenance safely and efficiently within the available timeslot
- Increase confidence and expertise in maintaining complex equipment

## Key tasks
- Diagnose issues
- Repair equipment
- Identify spare parts
- Access documentation
- Troubleshoot failures
- Verify compatibility
- Record maintenance work

## Needs
- Correct part identification
- Machine-specific documentation
- Installation procedure verification
- Troubleshooting guidance and support
- Part availability and lead-time visibility
- Updates on parts delivery dates for maintenance planning
- Training and maintenance knowledge to work safely and efficiently
- Confidence that the selected part will fit and solve the issue

## Pain points
- Uncertainty about correct part identification and selection
- Searching through multiple document sources
- Waiting for technical support responses
- Lack of machine-specific information
- Missing OEM maintenance and repair guidelines
- Limited visibility of spare-part availability, lead times, and arrival dates

## Jobs to be done
Repair equipment quickly and correctly.

## Work environment
Production floor, maintenance workshop, and machine area.
