# Quick-Change Blowhead Arms 200-202

- **Document type:** Technical news bulletin
- **Document ID:** TNB040RevA
- **Revision:** Rev A
- **Issued:** June 1995
- **Product family:** 200 series quick-change blowhead arms
- **Status:** Reference

## Summary

The 200 series standardises mold equipment through a common lock ring across the blowhead arm range. SG, DG, and TG arms use a standard lock-pin position at 45 degrees relative to the mold parting line.

## Compatibility table

| Machine type | Center distance | Threaded attachment | Bayonet attachment | Standard | Optional |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| E and EF 4 1/4 | DG 4 1/4 | 200-202 Gr. 12 | 200-202 Gr. 17 | 45 deg | 60 deg |
| E and EF 5 | DG 5 | 200-202 Gr. 13 | 200-202 Gr. 18 | 45 deg | 90 deg |
| F and EF 5 1/2 | DG 5 1/2 | 200-202 Gr. 14 | 200-202 Gr. 19 | 45 deg | 60 deg |
| F 6 1/4 and AIS | DG 6 1/4 | 200-202 Gr. 15 | 200-202 Gr. 20 | 45 deg | 60 deg |

## Installation note

Existing blowheads with vents between mold halves may require vent repositioning. DG arms include an additional lock-pin position to support existing mold equipment without modification.

## Ordering check

Confirm machine type, center distance, attachment type, lock position, and existing blowhead configuration before requesting a quotation.
