# Pantograph Baffle Arm

- **Document type:** Technical news bulletin
- **Document ID:** TNB033RevB
- **Revision:** Rev B
- **Issued:** October 2006
- **Product:** Pantograph Baffle Arm
- **Status:** Reference

## Summary

The Pantograph Baffle Arm improves alignment between blank molds and baffles, reduces force on the baffle mechanism by approximately 60 percent, and can prolong mechanism service life.

## Features

- Improved alignment between blank molds and baffles
- Improved motion and repeatability
- Approximately 60 percent less force on the mechanism
- No baffle arm overlap into the adjacent section in the off position
- Equal settle-blow pressure build-up in all cavities
- Quick-change baffle head

## Benefits

- Less wear on mold equipment
- Prolonged service life of the baffle mechanism
- Smoother baffle operation
- Reduced job-change time
- Quick conversion capability for SG, DG, and TG configurations
- Improved operating safety
- Compatibility with existing mold equipment

## Compatibility

The pantograph baffle is available for selected EF 5 1/2, AIS, F, and IS machine configurations. Verify the machine type, center distance, and quick-change baffle holder before ordering.

## Important part references

- Pantograph Baffle Arm: `210-208-1`
- Quick-change baffle holder references vary by machine and center distance.
- Installation may require drawing `210-214` and machine-specific modifications.

## Safety and installation note

Installation requires machine and baffle mechanism modifications. Confirm the applicable drawing and installation requirements with Emhart Glass before work begins.
